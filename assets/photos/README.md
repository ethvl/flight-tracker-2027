Destination banner photos, one per destination code: `TYO`, `KIX`, `OKA`, `SEL`, `CJU`, `PUS`
(`.jpg`, `.png` or `.webp`, landscape, any size: the build scales them down). Use photos you are allowed to reuse, for example from
Wikimedia Commons, and record the credit in `credits.json`:

    {"TYO": {"author": "Name", "license": "CC BY-SA 4.0", "url": "https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:..."}}

Credits are shown in the page footer. A destination without a photo shows a colour backdrop.
